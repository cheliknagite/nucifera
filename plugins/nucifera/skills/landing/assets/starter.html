Questions

Where does Seedbed get the forecast?

From open weather data for your town. The forecast is updated every morning at 06:00.

What if the forecast is wrong?

Seedbed advises with a margin: it waits for two warm nights after the last frost and checks the dates over 30 years. The decision is still yours.

Does it work for a greenhouse?

Yes. Seedbed counts a greenhouse and a cold frame separately: seedlings can go there earlier.

Do I pay every month?

No. One plot is free; a garden and a greenhouse cost $9 per season.
