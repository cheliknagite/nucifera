# Generic twin: states

How a default AI generator builds a 404, an empty result or an error screen:

- A huge "404" (120-200px) in gradient text with a glitch effect, or a stock cartoon (an astronaut, a broken robot, a lost cat).
- "Oops! Page not found 😢" and "The page you're looking for doesn't exist or has been moved." in 14px grey.
- A gradient "Go home" button with a glow, often a second "Contact support".
- Floating shapes, a bouncing illustration, a pulsing number.
- For an empty list: a big grey icon in a tinted circle, "No results found", "Try adjusting your filters", and no concrete suggestion.

**Named differences:**

1. **Brand material, not stock art.** Resend draws its own R with editor handles; Linear draws a small screen with a scrambled page; the twin uses a cartoon anyone could use.
2. **Small.** The exemplar drawings take 6.5% and 13% of the screen; the twin's "404" or illustration takes half.
3. **Named ways out.** Resend gives one button and 4 named links; the twin offers "Go home" only, and its empty state offers nothing concrete.
4. **Still.** Both exemplars have 0 animations; the twin bounces, glitches and pulses.
5. **Plain tone.** Two short lines without "Oops" or emoji.
