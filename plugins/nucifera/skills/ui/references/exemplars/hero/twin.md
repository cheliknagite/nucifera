# Generic twin: hero

How a default AI generator builds a landing or onboarding first screen with the same content:

- A centred stack: a pill badge "✨ New: AI-powered", an H1 at 56-72px, weight 700-800, tracking -0.04em or tighter, with one word in gradient text; a 16-18px grey subtitle; two buttons (a violet primary with a glow, a ghost secondary); "No credit card required" under them.
- Behind it: a large violet-blue radial blob and a dot grid; floating 3D shapes or a generic abstract render.
- Below: a phone or browser mockup with a blurred fake dashboard, tilted in 3D, with a glow under it.
- A "Trusted by" strip of grey logos.
- Everything fades up with the same 600 ms ease and a 100 ms stagger; the badge shimmers forever.

**Named differences:**

1. **The product is the hero.** Linear shows its real interface with believable data inside the first screen; the twin shows a blurred fake dashboard in a mockup.
2. **One owned object.** Things' crafted icon and Resend's dark cube belong to their products; the twin's blob could belong to anyone.
3. **One break in the scale, not gradient text.** Resend sets one serif headline in a sans page; the twin colours one word.
4. **No coloured light behind text.** None of the three exemplars puts a glow behind the headline; the twin's whole composition sits on a coloured blob.
5. **Fewer elements.** Resend's first screen has 5 text elements and one button, Things none at all; the twin stacks badge, headline, subtitle, two buttons, microcopy and logos.
6. **Motion in reading order.** Linear reveals headline, lead, then product (400-1300 ms) and nothing loops in view; Things does not move; the twin fades everything the same way and loops the badge.
