# Exemplars

Reference cards: real product screens at the level to aim for, each with measurements, the principles worth taking, what not to copy, and how it maps onto this kit. Each screen type also has a generic twin: the same content as a default AI generator would build it, with named differences.

The cards are text only: descriptions and measurements of public product pages observed in October 2026, with the source link. No screenshots are included; open the source to look. The products and their marks belong to their owners.

## How to use

1. Find the screen type below. Pick the two cards closest to your screen, at random among the close ones, and never just one. Read the type's `twin.md`.
2. Under the face in `.design/DESIGN.md`, write a transfer note: 3 principles you take and 3 things you do not take (block order, shapes, copy, the other product's signature, its colours).
3. Close the cards. Build from the note and the kit, not from the references.
4. Never build from a single reference, and never repeat a reference's block order or its signature: copies are recognised at once.
5. The critic later compares your render with the same two cards and the twin: closer to the cards or closer to the twin, and where the biggest gap is.
